1.Prize Description

One (1) winner will receive an Indian Scout Bobber motorcycle with an approximate retail value of $12,999.00 (the “Prize”).

The motorcycle will be redeemed through MotoPrimo, Lakeville, Minnesota. The winner will coordinate directly with MotoPrimo to arrange pickup and any optional upgrades or accessories.

2.Ticket Sales

  • Ticket Price: $100.00 per ticket.
  • Maximum Number of Tickets Available: 250 tickets.
  • Once all 250 tickets have been sold, no additional tickets will be issued.
  • Tickets will be sold on a first-come, first-served basis until sold out or until the drawing is held.
  • Sponsor reserves the right to end ticket sales at any time in accordance with Minnesota law.

3.Eligibility

  • Participants must be eighteen (18) years of age or older at the time of ticket purchase.
  • Tickets may only be purchased by individuals who are legally permitted to participate under applicable laws.
  • Directors, officers, board members, volunteers, and employees of KSP Gives Back are eligible to participate unless otherwise prohibited by Minnesota law.

4.Payment Methods

  • Payment must be made in full at the time of purchase.
  • Cash or check payable to KSP Gives Back are accepted payment methods.
  • No refunds will be issued except as required by law or at the sole discretion of the Sponsor if the raffle is canceled.

5.Drawing Date and Procedure

  • The raffle drawing is scheduled to be held on Tuesday, August 11, 2026, at 6:00 PM Central Time.
  • The drawing may be broadcast or livestreamed by KSP Gives Back.
  • Participants do not need to be present to win.
  • The drawing will be conducted on the scheduled drawing date regardless of the number of tickets sold. Any revised drawing date will be publicly announced through KSP Gives Back communication channels and website.

6.Winner Selection and Notification

  • One winning ticket will be selected at random from all eligible tickets sold.
  • For privacy and security purposes, the winner’s full name will not be announced publicly during the livestream or public announcements.
  • The winning ticket number, along with the winner’s first name and last initial, will be announced.
  • KSP Gives Back representatives will make reasonable efforts to contact the winner using the contact information provided at the time of ticket purchase. The initial notification call is expected to be made no later than 6:10 PM Central Time on the date of the drawing.
  • If the winner cannot be reached immediately, KSP Gives Back will continue reasonable efforts to establish contact.

7.Prize Redemption

  • The winner must coordinate prize pickup directly with MotoPrimo in Lakeville, Minnesota.
  • The motorcycle must be accepted “as awarded” and may not be exchanged for cash or substituted for another model, except at the sole discretion of the Sponsor if circumstances beyond its control make the advertised prize unavailable.
  • Any optional upgrades, accessories, or modifications requested by the winner shall be arranged directly with MotoPrimo and shall be the sole financial responsibility of the winner.

8.Taxes, Fees, and Additional Costs

  • The winner is solely responsible for all applicable federal, state, and local taxes, title fees, registration fees, licensing costs, sales tax, insurance, and any other expenses associated with accepting and taking possession of the Prize.
  • Payment of all applicable taxes, fees, and any optional upgrade charges is due at the time the motorcycle is picked up from MotoPrimo.
  • KSP Gives Back and MotoPrimo are not responsible for any costs beyond providing the awarded motorcycle.

9.Odds of Winning

The odds of winning depend on the total number of eligible tickets sold. If all tickets are sold, the odds of winning are 1 in 250.

10.Publicity and Privacy

By accepting the Prize, the winner grants KSP Gives Back permission to use the winner’s first name, last initial, and ticket number, for the initial announcement. With the winner's consent, a potential likeness may be used for promotional purposes related to the raffle without additional compensation, except where prohibited by law.  KSP Gives Back will not publicly disclose the winner’s full name without the winner’s consent.

11.Release of Liability

By participating, entrants agree to release, discharge, and hold harmless KSP Gives Back, its board members, officers, volunteers, sponsors, and affiliates, including MotoPrimo, from any and all claims, losses, injuries, damages, or liabilities arising out of participation in the raffle or acceptance, use, or misuse of the Prize.

12.Sponsor’s Right to Modify or Cancel

KSP Gives Back reserves the right to amend these Official Rules, postpone the drawing, or cancel the raffle if necessary due to circumstances beyond its reasonable control or as required by applicable law. In the event of cancellation, refunds will be handled in accordance with Minnesota law.

13.Compliance with Law

This raffle is conducted pursuant to Minnesota charitable gambling laws and regulations. Any dispute arising under these Official Rules shall be governed by the laws of the State of Minnesota.
